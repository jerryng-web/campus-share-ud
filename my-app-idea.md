\f0\fs24 \cf0 # CampusShare (UD Edition)\
\
## One-Sentence Summary\
CampusShare helps University of Delaware students borrow and lend items within their residential complexes, maximizing limited dorm spaces and saving money on tight student budgets.\
\
## The Problem\
UD residence halls and student apartments have incredibly tight space limits. Buying a vacuum, air mattress, clothing iron, or specialized tools that you only use once a month takes up valuable floor space and drains a tight student budget. Meanwhile, another student down the hall might have those exact items sitting idle. Group chats get chaotic, and there is no reliable way to see who has what available to borrow across the UD campus.\
\
## User Types\
- **Registered Users**: All users have the same capabilities. They can list items they are willing to lend and request to borrow items from others.\
\
## Core Features\
- **List an Item**: Post an item available for borrowing with a title, description, category (e.g., Electronics, Appliances, Tools), and selected UD Residence Complex.\
- **Browse & Filter Items**: View available items and filter them by item category or specific UD Campus Geographic Areas.\
- **Request to Borrow**: Click to request an item for a specific date range.\
- **Manage Requests**: View incoming requests from other students to approve or deny them.\
- **Return Tracking**: Mark a borrowed item as safely returned to clear the log.\
\
## UD Campus Location Scope\
To ensure convenience, items are mapped directly to official UD Residence Complexes:\
1. **Laird Campus Suites** (George Read, Independence, James Smith, Thomas McKean)\
2. **East Campus Traditional & Z-Halls** (Caesar Rodney, Eliphalet Gilbert, Harrington A-E, Lane, Louis L. Redding, Russell A-D)\
3. **Central & South Campus** (North Central, South Central, Smyth, South Academy, Ray Street A-B)\
4. **Apartment Complexes** (University Courtyard, Graduate Student Housing)\
\
## What Users See\
\
### Screen 1: Dashboard / Browse Items\
A clean inventory feed showing:\
- Item name and category icon.\
- Owner's specific UD Complex (e.g., "Laird Campus Suites - George Read").\
- Current availability status ("Available" or "On Loan").\
- Dropdown filters to isolate items only on your specific campus area (e.g., "East Campus").\
- A "Request to Borrow" button.\
\
### Screen 2: List Your Stuff\
A simple input form with fields for:\
- Item Name (e.g., "George Foreman Grill", "Cordless Drill").\
- Category selection dropdown.\
- UD Location Selector (Dropdown choosing the specific Residence Complex and building).\
- Brief description and pick-up instructions.\
\
### Screen 3: Item & Request Details\
A focused view showing:\
- Full item rules and owner notes.\
- A date picker input field to choose the borrow duration.\
- An action history box tracking who is currently borrowing it.\
\
### Screen 4: My Locker (Dashboard)\
A dual-list view showing:\
- "Items I am Borrowing" (with return buttons).\
- "Items I am Lending" (with pending requests from peers to approve or deny).\
\
## Example Scenario\
Alex moves into a compact room in Harrington Hall (East Campus) and needs a garment steamer for a presentation on Friday morning, but doesn't want to spend $30 or waste closet space buying one. Alex opens CampusShare, filters by "Appliances" and "East Campus", and finds that Sam has a steamer listed as available in Caesar Rodney Hall. \
\
Alex clicks "Request to Borrow" for Thursday night. Sam receives a notification on their dashboard under "My Locker" and clicks "Approve". They meet up, Alex uses it, and clicks "Mark as Returned" on Friday afternoon after dropping it back off.\
